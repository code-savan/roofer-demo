FAQ

The questions we hear on every doorstep.

Hail is an “act of nature,” so claims are generally no-fault and don't raise your rate the way an at-fault auto claim might. What insurers do watch is claim frequency across a zip code — and after a big DFW hail event, half the neighborhood is filing at once. Our take: if the damage is real, use the coverage you already pay for. We'll give you an honest read before you ever file.

Three things decide it: the age of the roof, how much of it is damaged (insurers often apply a 25–30% threshold), and whether the decking underneath is still sound. About one in five of our inspections ends with “you're fine for a few more years” — and we put that in writing, free of charge.

Most DFW homes: one day. Tear-off in the morning, dry-in by lunch, shingles and cleanup by dinner. Tile, metal, or steep cut-up roofs can take two to three days. Whatever the timeline, we never leave a roof unprotected overnight.

Pea-size (¼″) almost never. Quarter-size (1″) starts bruising shingles that are 10–15 years old. Golf-ball (1¾″) damages almost anything — and that's routine in North Texas. If granules are filling your gutters after a storm, that's your roof asking for a free look.

Yes. We photograph and document to carrier standards, meet your adjuster on the roof, file supplements when the scope falls short, and walk you through every line before you sign anything. You make one phone call — to us.

Out-of-state plates, a hotel-room “office,” high-pressure “today only” pricing, and promises to “eat your deductible” — which is fraud and can jeopardize your whole claim. A real local roofer has a real address, real license numbers, and will still be here to honor the warranty in year ten.
